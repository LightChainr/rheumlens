## Innovation boundary supported by these references

The paper should not claim invention of patient-level prediction, set pooling,
donor-aware cross-validation, external SLE validation, or the general observation
that batch-label confounding biases cross-validation. Its defensible first claim is
the **combined empirical test**: two SLE cohorts, identical donors and classifier
pipeline, design-only prediction, representation-to-design prediction,
fold-contained residualisation, design restriction with size/label-matched controls,
cross-batch transfer, cross-cohort transfer, and learned pooling. FloREN is the
closest 2026 collision, but it does not report this validity chain.
