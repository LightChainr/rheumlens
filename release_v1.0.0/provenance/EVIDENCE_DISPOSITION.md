# Evidence disposition for the figure-rich draft

## Primary evidence retained in the central narrative

- Twenty repeated stratified five-fold donor-level internal evaluations in three cohorts.
- Reciprocal source-only transfer between the two CD4-positive cohorts.
- Paired target-donor inference with multiplicity correction.
- Source-only early fusion and the direct cap500 pooling stress test.
- Source-size learning curves and matched Geneformer cell-budget analyses.
- Repeated interferon conditioning and matched expression-module sensitivity.

## New traceable results added to the working manuscript

- Shared source-internal fold sensitivity for the three primary transfer representations.
- Cap500 donor-mean geometry, sampled-cell spectral rank, and cap500-cap1000 geometry preservation.
- A fully vectorized three-cohort donor-pseudobulk volcano and high-effect overlap group figure.

## Supporting evidence retained with explicit boundaries

- Repeated scGPT low-cell and aggregation analyses use archived, distinct estimands.
- The 22-method pooling atlas is a fixed-split single-cohort comparison.
- The historical multicohort matrix combines heterogeneous implementation branches and missing cells.
- Covariate residualization and older nuisance probes are archived sensitivities, not matched primary analyses.
- The low-rank geometry is descriptive and does not establish a causal mechanism.

## Excluded from confirmatory claims

- Incomplete configuration grids as evidence of model-size superiority.
- Target-label-selected transfer transformations.
- Unmatched whole-PBMC composition as a CD4-only baseline.
- Clinical deployment, diagnostic readiness, formal cell-count sufficiency, or IFN-specific causal claims.
